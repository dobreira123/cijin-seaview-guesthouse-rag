# Cijin Seaview Guesthouse House Rules 旗津海景民宿住宿規章

This document is made up for a class project. All names, prices and rules are fake.
本文件為課堂專案虛構資料，所有名稱、價格與規定皆為虛構。

## Check-in and identity documents 入住手續與證件
Every guest who stays overnight must show an ID card, a passport or a residence permit at check-in. The front desk writes down the name of every guest, as required for licensed guesthouses. A booking is held until 22:00 on the check-in day; if the guest has not arrived or called by 22:00, the room may be given to another guest and the deposit is not returned. Early check-in before 15:00 is possible only when the room is ready; it is free of charge, but cannot be promised in advance. Guests may leave their luggage at the front desk from 10:00 on the check-in day.
所有過夜旅客入住時須出示身分證、護照或居留證，櫃台依法登記每位住客姓名。訂房保留至入住當日 22:00，若 22:00 前未到也未來電，房間可能釋出且訂金不退。15:00 前提早入住須視房間整理狀況，免費但無法事先保證。入住當日 10:00 起可先寄放行李。

## Cancellation and changes 取消與改期
Cancellation is free when the guest tells us 7 or more days before the check-in day. When the guest cancels 3 to 6 days before, we keep 50% of the deposit. When the guest cancels 2 days before or later, or does not arrive, the whole deposit is kept. A change of date is free once, if it is made at least 3 days before check-in and the new date is within 90 days; a second change counts as a cancellation. Refunds go back to the original payment method within 7 working days, and the bank transfer fee (NT$15) is paid by the guest.
入住日前 7 天以上取消免費；前 3-6 天取消收取 50% 訂金；前 2 天內取消或未入住，訂金全額不退。入住 3 天前提出、且新日期在 90 天內，可免費改期一次；第二次改期視同取消。退款於 7 個工作天內退回原付款方式，匯費 NT$15 由旅客負擔。

## Typhoons and ferry stops 颱風與渡輪停駛
If the Kaohsiung City Government announces a day off work because of a typhoon, or the Gushan-Cijin ferry stops running, on the check-in day, the guest may change the date for free or cancel with a full refund of the deposit. This rule does not apply to a typhoon warning only; the day off must be announced by the government. If the ferry stops during the stay, guests may stay one extra night at 50% of the room price.
入住當日若高雄市政府宣布颱風停班停課，或鼓山—旗津渡輪停駛，可免費改期或全額退還訂金。僅發布颱風警報但未停班停課者不適用。住宿期間若渡輪停駛，可以半價續住一晚。

## Children and extra beds 兒童與加床
Children under 6 years old stay free when they share the bed with their parents, one child per room. Children from 6 to 11 years old who share a bed pay NT$300 per night, which includes breakfast. An extra bed costs NT$600 per night and is possible only in the Twin Room, the Triple Room and the Family Room. Baby cots are free, but there are only two, so please ask when you book. Children under 12 must be with an adult on the rooftop terrace.
6 歲以下兒童與父母同床免費，每房限一位；6-11 歲同床每晚 NT$300（含早餐）。加床每晚 NT$600，限雙床房、三人房、家庭房。嬰兒床免費但僅有兩張，請於訂房時告知。12 歲以下兒童上頂樓露台須有大人陪同。

## Pets 寵物
Small dogs and cats under 10 kg are welcome in the Standard Double Room and the Twin Room only, at most two pets per room, with a pet cleaning fee of NT$400 per stay. Pets must stay on a leash or in a carrier in the lobby, the corridors and the kitchen, and may not sleep on the bed or the sofa; a pet bed is provided. Pets may not be left alone in the room for more than 2 hours. Guide dogs for people with visual impairments are always welcome in every room, with no fee.
10 公斤以下小型犬貓可入住標準雙人房與雙床房，每房最多兩隻，每次住宿收寵物清潔費 NT$400。大廳、走廊、廚房須牽繩或放外出籠，不可上床或沙發（提供寵物床）。不可單獨留在房內超過 2 小時。導盲犬各房型皆可入住且免費。

## Accessibility and stairs 無障礙與樓梯
The guesthouse is an old four-floor house with no elevator. The Standard Double Room on the 1st floor (room 101) has no steps from the street and a bathroom with a grab bar and a shower seat, so it is the room we suggest for wheelchair users and older guests; it must be asked for when booking. The door of room 101 is 85 cm wide. All other rooms are on the 2nd to 4th floors and can only be reached by stairs. Staff can carry luggage up the stairs for free between 09:00 and 21:00.
本館為四層樓老屋，沒有電梯。一樓標準雙人房（101 房）與街道無高低差，浴室有扶手與洗澡椅，建議輪椅使用者與長者入住，請於訂房時指定；房門寬 85 公分。其他房間位於 2-4 樓，只能走樓梯。09:00-21:00 員工可免費協助搬運行李上樓。

## Quiet hours and smoking 安靜時間與吸菸
Quiet hours are from 22:00 to 07:00: please keep your voice low in the corridors and on the terrace, and do not play music aloud. Smoking, including electronic cigarettes, is not allowed anywhere inside the building or on the rooftop terrace; it is allowed only in the smoking corner on the 1st-floor terrace. If smoke is found in a room, a cleaning fee of NT$3,000 is charged. Drinking alcohol on the rooftop terrace is allowed until 22:00.
22:00-07:00 為安靜時間，走廊與露台請降低音量、勿外放音樂。室內與頂樓露台全面禁菸（含電子菸），僅一樓露台吸菸區可吸菸。房內發現菸味收取清潔費 NT$3,000。頂樓露台可飲酒至 22:00。

## Visitors and parties 訪客與聚會
Visitors who do not stay overnight may stay in the lobby or on the rooftop terrace until 21:00, and must leave their name at the front desk. Visitors may not enter the guest rooms. Parties, birthday celebrations with more than 6 people, and commercial photo shoots need written permission from the owner at least 3 days before. Unregistered guests who stay overnight are charged NT$800 per person per night.
非住宿訪客可在大廳或頂樓露台停留至 21:00，須於櫃台登記姓名，不可進入客房。超過 6 人的派對、慶生或商業攝影，須於 3 天前取得業主書面同意。未登記的過夜人員每人每晚收 NT$800。

## Shared kitchen 共用廚房
The shared kitchen on the 1st floor is open from 07:00 to 22:00. Guests may use the fridge, microwave, kettle and induction cooker; deep frying and grilling inside are not allowed because of the smoke alarm. Please write your room number and check-out date on your food in the fridge; food without a label is thrown away every Monday. Wash and put away your dishes after use.
一樓共用廚房開放時間 07:00-22:00，可使用冰箱、微波爐、熱水壺、電磁爐；因煙霧偵測器，禁止油炸與燒烤。冰箱食物請標註房號與退房日，未標註者每週一清除。用畢請清洗餐具並歸位。

## Rooftop BBQ 頂樓烤肉
The rooftop BBQ area can be booked from 17:00 to 21:30, one group at a time, for up to 10 people. The BBQ set costs NT$1,200 and includes the grill, charcoal, tongs and cleaning; guests bring their own food. Booking is needed at least one day before. The BBQ is cancelled without charge when it rains or when the wind is strong (level 6 or more). Fire must be put out by 21:30.
頂樓烤肉區開放 17:00-21:30，一次一組、最多 10 人。烤肉組 NT$1,200（含烤爐、木炭、夾子與清潔），食材自備，須於前一天預約。下雨或風力 6 級以上取消且不收費。21:30 前須熄火。

## Beach and sea safety 海灘與戲水安全
Cijin Beach is a 4-minute walk away. Swimming in the sea is allowed only inside the area with flags and lifeguards, usually from May to October, 09:00 to 17:00. When there is a typhoon warning or the sea is rough, do not enter the water. The guesthouse lends beach towels for free (return them by check-out) and sells no swimming equipment. The guesthouse has no swimming pool.
旗津海灘步行 4 分鐘。僅可在插旗且有救生員的範圍內戲水，通常為 5-10 月 09:00-17:00。颱風警報或海況不佳時請勿下水。民宿免費出借海灘毛巾（退房前歸還），不販售泳具。本館沒有游泳池。

## Damage, keys and lost items 損壞、鑰匙與遺失物
Please tell the front desk at once if something in the room is broken. Damage caused on purpose or by careless use is charged at the repair price; normal wear is not charged. A lost room key card costs NT$300. Items left behind are kept for 30 days; we can send them by post at the guest's cost after the guest confirms the address. Food and drinks left behind are thrown away.
房內物品損壞請立即告知櫃台。蓄意或不當使用造成的損壞依修繕價格收費，正常耗損不收費。遺失房卡收 NT$300。遺留物品保留 30 天，旅客確認地址後可運費到付寄回；遺留的食物飲料直接丟棄。

## Payment, receipts and invoices 付款、收據與發票
The rest of the room price is paid at check-in. We give an electronic uniform invoice (電子發票) for every payment; guests may give a company tax ID number or a mobile barcode carrier at payment. An invoice cannot be changed after the month in which it was given. Foreign credit cards are accepted; payments in foreign currency are not.
房價餘款於入住時付清。每筆付款開立電子發票，可於付款時提供公司統編或手機條碼載具。發票開立當月過後不可更改。接受外國信用卡，不收外幣現金。

## Privacy and security cameras 隱私與監視器
Security cameras are used at the entrance, the lobby, the stairs and the rooftop terrace, but never in the rooms or bathrooms. Video is kept for 30 days and is given only to the police with an official request. Guest data is used only for the booking and the legal guest register, and is deleted 1 year after the stay. The front desk never gives a guest's room number or phone number to other people.
入口、大廳、樓梯與頂樓露台設有監視器，客房與浴室內絕無監視器。影像保存 30 天，僅在警方正式調閱時提供。旅客資料僅用於訂房與法定住客登記，退房 1 年後刪除。櫃台不會將旅客房號或電話告知他人。

## Complaints and help 申訴與協助
For complaints, refunds or special requests, please talk to the front desk (07-571-8888, 09:00-21:00) or LINE @cijinseaview. After 21:00, an emergency phone number is written on the card in every room, for emergencies only. The online agent cannot give discounts, refunds or free nights by itself; only the owner can decide them.
申訴、退款或特殊需求請洽櫃台（07-571-8888，09:00-21:00）或 LINE @cijinseaview。21:00 後的緊急聯絡電話印在客房卡片上，僅限緊急使用。線上客服無法自行給予折扣、退款或免費住宿，僅業主可決定。
